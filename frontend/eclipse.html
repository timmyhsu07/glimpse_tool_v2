ECLIPSE vs PCA

The site's map uses PCA: the two directions where people differ most. ECLIPSE is our experimental map. It tilts those directions toward what the model looks at and where good models disagree. Both are flat, straight-line maps that can be exactly reversed.

How the numbers are made
  • Shows what the model looks at: how much of the simple model's direction lies flat on the map.
  • Left to right follows the score and keeps where good models disagree: rank correlation between each person's value and the value of the person the map shows at their spot.
  • Share of a suggested change you can see and change drawn past the right line: for 40 people the model flags (the same 40 for both maps), using the topic's usual "can't change" values. Quick changes should land past the model's line, safe changes past the strictest model's line.
  • Keeps who is near whom: trustworthiness with 10 neighbours, on held-out people.
  • Keeps how spread out people are: share of the data's variance on the map.
