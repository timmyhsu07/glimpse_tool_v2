How it works

  1. 1Pick a topic Diabetes or breast cancer.
  2. 2Enter your numbers Or click anywhere on the map.
  3. 3See where you stand How sure the models are, and how you compare.
  4. 4See what would change it The smallest changes you can actually make.
Why “how sure”?

Many different models fit the same data about equally well, but they can give one person different answers. GLIMPSE shows the whole range, so a close call looks like a close call.

The map squeezes everyone onto a flat page. Areas where many people sit look darker. Click any spot to see the person who would be there.
