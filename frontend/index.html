THE RESEARCH QUESTION

How reliable is a prediction when good models disagree?

Traditional explanations often focus on one model and one answer. GLIMPSE lets you inspect a range of good models, explore where their predictions differ, and examine possible counterfactual changes—without treating a model output as certainty.

How it works

  4. 4See what would change it The smallest changes you can actually make.
Why “how sure”?

Many different models fit the same data about equally well, but they can give one person different answers. GLIMPSE shows the whole range, so a close call looks like a close call.

The map squeezes everyone onto a flat page. Areas where many people sit look darker. Click any spot to see the person who would be there.

GLIMPSE is a research and educational prototype. Model outputs are estimates, not ground truth, and should not be used as medical, financial, or other professional advice. Read about assumptions and limitations.
