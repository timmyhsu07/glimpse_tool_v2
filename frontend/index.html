      About this view

      Where do you stand? A model sorts people into two groups. This tool shows where a person lands, how sure the model really is once you account for every equally-good model, and the smallest realistic change that would move them — with a simple and a flexible model side by side.

      Each dot is a person; ★ is the selected person; ✕ and ▪ are the counterfactuals and the arrow points to the robust change. A simple straight-line model and a flexible neural net are shown side by side. Click any dot to inspect that person, or click an empty spot to see the person the map places there.
