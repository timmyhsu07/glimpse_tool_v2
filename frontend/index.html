What's different

Most “what would change it” tools

  • Trust one model's answer.
  • Suggest a change that flips that one model.
  • Show what mattered to that one model.

GLIMPSE

  • Checks every model that fits the data about as well.
  • Shows the range of answers, so a close call looks close.
  • Offers a safe change that works for every good model.
  • Shows when good models disagree about what mattered.
